How it works

No typing. No English required. No new app.

  1. 1. Speak or snap

    Send a voice note or photo in Hausa, Yoruba, or Igbo — right inside WhatsApp.

  2. 2. AjaTrade understands

    Powered by Llama 3 and Llama Vision, it listens, sees, and reasons in your language.

  3. 3. Get answers by voice

    Advice, fair pricing, or loan-ready documents come back as voice notes, text, and PDFs.
